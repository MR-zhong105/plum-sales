# 一般现在时综合测试

## 试题内容（简版）

### 一、单项选择（20题）
1. She ___ (go) to school by bus every day.
2. My mother ___ (cook) dinner every evening.
3. ___ your brother ___ (like) football?
4. Tom ___ (not play) basketball on weekdays.
5. The sun ___ (rise) in the east.

### 二、用所给动词形式填空（10题）
6-15. read, play, go, study, watch, not like, do, have, be, live

### 三、翻译句子（5题）
16. 我每天7点起床。
17. 他不喜欢吃肉。
18. 她每周六去图书馆。
19. 你爸爸是老师吗？
20. 我们学校有2000名学生。

## 参考答案
1. goes  2. cooks  3. Does; like  4. doesn't play  5. rises
16. I get up at 7 every day.  17. He doesn't like meat.  18. She goes to the library every Saturday.  19. Is your father a teacher?  20. Our school has 2000 students.
