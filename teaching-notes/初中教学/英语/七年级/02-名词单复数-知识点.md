# 名词单复数

> 七年级英语核心语法

## 知识内容

### 1. 规则变化

| 规则 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 一般加-s | book→books, bag→bags, pen→pens |
| 以s,x,ch,sh结尾加-es | box→boxes, watch→watches, bus→buses |
| 以o结尾：有生命加-es，无生命加-s | tomato→tomatoes, potato→potatoes；photo→photos, radio→radios |
| 辅音+y结尾，变y为i加-es | baby→babies, city→cities |
| 以f/fe结尾，变f/fe为v加-es | leaf→leaves, wolf→wolves, knife→knives |

### 2. 不规则变化

| 单数 | 复数 | 单数 | 复数 |
|------|------|------|------|
| man | men | woman | women |
| foot | feet | tooth | teeth |
| child | children | person | people |
| mouse | mice | goose | geese |

### 3. 单复数同形

deer→deer, sheep→sheep, fish→fish（鱼类的条数）

### 4. 只作复数的名词

people, clothes, shoes, glasses, trousers, scissors, socks

### 5. 集合名词

family（家庭整体/家庭成员）、class（班级整体/班级成员）、team

## 典型例题

**例题1**：用所给名词的复数形式填空
1. There are many ___ (child) playing in the park.
2. I need two pairs of ___ (glass).
3. The ___ (sheep) are eating grass on the hill.
4. My ___ (foot) are very big.

**答案**：1. children  2. glasses  3. sheep  4. feet

**例题2**：选择正确答案
(  ) There are two ___ in the room.
A. woman teacher  B. women teachers  C. woman teachers  D. women teacher
**答案**：B

## 易错点提醒

### 易错点1：不规则变化记混
**正确记忆**：man→men, woman→women, foot→feet, tooth→teeth

### 易错点2：复合名词复数
复合名词变复数时，变主要部分：
- 有中心词：boy→boys, teacher→teachers
- 无中心词：man/woman作定语：man doctor→men doctors

### 易错点3：只作复数的名词
people（人/人们）、clothes（衣服）不能加s
