# 一般过去时专项练习

> 七年级英语语法核心考点

## 知识内容

### 1. 一般过去时结构

**肯定句**：主语 + 动词过去式
- I went to school yesterday.
- She played basketball last Sunday.

**否定句**：主语 + did not (didn't) + 动词原形
- I didn't go to school yesterday.
- She didn't play basketball last Sunday.

**一般疑问句**：Did + 主语 + 动词原形?
- Did you go to school yesterday?
- Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

**特殊疑问句**：疑问词 + did + 主语 + 动词原形?
- What did you do yesterday?
- Where did she go?

### 2. 动词过去式变化规则

**规则变化**：
- 一般加-ed：play→played, work→worked
- 以e结尾加-d：live→lived, use→used
- 辅音+y结尾，变y为i加-ed：study→studied, carry→carried
- 重读闭音节，双写末尾辅音加-ed：stop→stopped, plan→planned

**不规则变化**（常考）：
| 原形 | 过去式 | 原形 | 过去式 |
|------|--------|------|--------|
| go | went | eat | ate |
| see | saw | take | took |
| come | came | get | got |
| do | did | buy | bought |
| have | had | make | made |
| write | wrote | swim | swam |
| drink | drank | sing | sang |
| read | read /red/ | run | ran |
| know | knew | find | found |
| think | thought | tell | told |

### 3. 时间标志词

yesterday, last night/week/year, ago, in 2020, just now, when I was young

### 4. be动词过去式

- I/he/she/it + was
- You/we/they + were

### 5. 练习

**用所给动词的过去式填空**：
1. I ___ (go) to the park yesterday.
2. She ___ (not do) her homework last night.
3. ___ you ___ (see) the movie last weekend?
4. Tom ___ (buy) a new bike two days ago.
5. We ___ (have) a great time at the party.

**答案**：1. went  2. didn't do  3. Did / see  4. bought  5. had

## 易错点提醒

### 易错点1：did后面动词忘记还原
**正确**：Did you went? → Did you go?

### 易错点2：不规则动词过去式记错
**正确**：go→went, see→saw, buy→bought
