# 形容词与副词比较级和最高级

> 八年级英语核心语法

## 知识内容

### 1. 比较级（两者比较）

| 类型 | 规则 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 单音节词 | 加-er | tall→taller, fast→faster |
| 以e结尾 | 加-r | nice→nicer, large→larger |
| 辅音+y结尾 | 变y为i加-er | happy→happier, busy→busier |
| 重读闭音节 | 双写末尾+er | big→bigger, thin→thinner |
| 多音节词 | 前面加more | beautiful→more beautiful |

**结构**：A + 动词 + 比较级 + **than** + B

### 2. 最高级（三者及以上比较）

| 类型 | 规则 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 单音节词 | 加-est | tall→tallest, fast→fastest |
| 以e结尾 | 加-st | nice→nicest |
| 辅音+y结尾 | 变y为i加-est | happy→happiest |
| 重读闭音节 | 双写末尾+est | big→biggest |
| 多音节词 | 前面加most | beautiful→most beautiful |

**结构**：the + 最高级 + **in**（范围）/ **of**（范围）

### 3. 特殊变化

| 原级 | 比较级 | 最高级 |
|------|--------|--------|
| good/well | better | best |
| bad/badly | worse | worst |
| many/much | more | most |
| little | less | least |
| far | farther/further | farthest/furthest |

### 4. 常用句型

- **the + 比较级..., the + 比较级...**：越...越...
  - The more you practice, the better you will be.
- **more and more + 形容词**：越来越...
  - The weather is getting more and more warm.
- **比较级 + and + 比较级**：越来越...
  - It's getting colder and colder.

### 5. as...as 结构

- **肯定**：as + 原级 + as（和...一样）
  - She is as tall as her sister.
- **否定**：not as/so + 原级 + as（不如...）
  - He is not as tall as his brother.

## 典型例题

**例题1**：用所给形容词的正确形式填空
1. Tom is ___ (tall) than Jack.
2. This is the ___ (interesting) book I have ever read.
3. She is ___ (good) at English than her brother.
4. The weather in summer is ___ (hot) than in spring.

**答案**：1. taller  2. most interesting  3. better  4. hotter

**例题2**：（中考题）— Who is ___ (young) in your class? — Li Hua is.
**答案**：the youngest

**例题3**：选择正确答案
(  ) The Great Wall is one of ___ (famous) places in the world.
A. famous  B. more famous  C. the most famous  D. most famous
**答案**：C

## 易错点提醒

### 易错点1：比较级前面加more或-er同时使用
**错误**：more taller
**正确**：taller（比较级只用一种形式）

### 易错点2：最高级忘记加the
**错误**：Tallest building in the city
**正确**：**The** tallest building in the city

### 易错点3：不规则变化记错
**正确记忆**：good→better→best, bad→worse→worst
