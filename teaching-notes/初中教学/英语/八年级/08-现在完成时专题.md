# 现在完成时专题

> 八年级英语语法核心考点，中考必考

## 知识内容

### 1. 现在完成时结构

**肯定句**：主语 + have/has + 过去分词
- I have finished my homework.
- She has been to Beijing.

**否定句**：主语 + have/has not + 过去分词
- I haven't finished my homework.
- She hasn't been to Beijing.

**一般疑问句**：Have/Has + 主语 + 过去分词?
- Have you finished your homework?
- Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

**特殊疑问句**：疑问词 + have/has + 主语 + 过去分词?
- What have you done?
- Where has she gone?

### 2. 现在完成时的用法

**用法1**：过去发生的动作对现在有影响
- I have lost my key.（我现在没有钥匙）
- He has finished his work.（他完成了工作）

**用法2**：从过去持续到现在的动作或状态
- I have lived here for 10 years.
- She has studied English since 2020.

**用法3**：过去的经验
- I have been to Shanghai.（我去过上海）
- Have you ever eaten Japanese food?

### 3. for 和 since 的用法

| 标志词 | 用法 | 示例 |
|--------|------|------|
| for | 后接时间段 | for two years, for a long time |
| since | 后接时间点 | since 2020, since yesterday |

**注意**：for + 时间段 = since + 时间点
- for two years = since two years ago
- for ten days = since ten days ago

### 4. already, yet, ever, never

| 词 | 用法 | 位置 |
|----|------|------|
| already | 已经（肯定句） | 句中或句末 |
| yet | 还（否定句、疑问句） | 句末 |
| ever | 曾经（疑问句） | 句中 |
| never | 从不（否定句） | 句中 |

**示例**：
- I have already finished my homework.
- Have you finished your homework yet?
- I have never been to Japan.
- Have you ever seen a tiger?

### 5. have been to vs have gone to

| 表达 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| have/has been to | 去过（已回来） | I have been to Beijing. |
| have/has gone to | 去了（未回来） | He has gone to Beijing. |

### 6. 现在完成时 vs 一般过去时

| 对比项 | 现在完成时 | 一般过去时 |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| 时间 | 不强调具体时间 | 强调具体过去时间 |
| 标志词 | already, yet, ever, never, for, since | yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020 |
| 侧重 | 对现在的影响 | 过去发生的动作 |

**示例**：
- I have lost my key.（我现在没钥匙）
- I lost my key yesterday.（昨天丢了）

### 7. 练习

**用现在完成时填空**：
1. I ___ (finish) my homework already.
2. She ___ (not read) the book yet.
3. ___ you ever ___ (be) to London?
4. We ___ (know) each other for ten years.
5. He ___ (go) to Shanghai. He'll be back next week.

**答案**：1. have finished  2. hasn't read  3. Have / been  4. have known  5. has gone

## 易错点提醒

### 易错点1：与一般过去时混用
**正确**：有具体过去时间用一般过去时，强调对现在影响用现在完成时

### 易错点2：already/yet位置错误
**正确**：already在助动词后实义动词前；yet在句末
