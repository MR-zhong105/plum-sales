# 一般过去时

> 八年级英语核心语法

## 知识内容

### 1. 定义

表示过去某个时间发生的动作或存在的状态。

### 2. 结构

| 类型 | 结构 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 肯定句 | 主语 + 动词过去式 | I went to school yesterday. |
| 否定句 | 主语 + did not (didn't) + 动词原形 | I didn't go to school yesterday. |
| 一般疑问句 | Did + 主语 + 动词原形 | Did you go to school yesterday? |
| 特殊疑问句 | 疑问词 + did + 主语 + 动词原形 | Where did you go? |

### 3. 规则动词过去式变化

| 规则 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 一般加-ed | play→played, work→worked |
| 以e结尾加-d | live→lived, use→used |
| 辅音+y结尾，变y为i加-ed | study→studied, carry→carried |
| 重读闭音节，双写末尾辅音+ed | stop→stopped, plan→planned |

### 4. 不规则动词过去式（常考）

| 原形 | 过去式 | 原形 | 过去式 |
|------|--------|------|--------|
| be | was/were | go | went |
| do | did | eat | ate |
| have/has | had | see | saw |
| come | came | take | took |
| get | got | buy | bought |
| make | made | read | read /red/ |
| write | wrote | swim | swam |
| drink | drank | sing | sang |

### 5. 时间标志词

yesterday, last night/week/year, ago（...以前）, in 2020, just now, when I was young

## 典型例题

**例题1**：用所给动词的过去式填空
1. I ___ (go) to Beijing last summer.
2. She ___ (not do) her homework yesterday.
3. ___ you ___ (see) the movie last night?
4. Tom ___ (buy) a new bike two days ago.

**答案**：1. went  2. didn't do  3. Did / see  4. bought

**例题2**：（中考题）— Where ___ you ___ (be) last Sunday? — I ___ (stay) at home.
**答案**：were; be; stayed

## 易错点提醒

### 易错点1：did后面动词忘记还原
**错误**：Did you went to school?
**正确**：Did you go to school?

### 易错点2：不规则动词过去式记错
**正确记忆**：go→went, eat→ate, see→saw, buy→bought

### 易错点3：一般过去时与一般现在时混淆
**错误**：I go to school yesterday.
**正确**：I went to school yesterday.
